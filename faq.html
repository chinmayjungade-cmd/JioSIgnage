Overview6

What hardware do I need to get started?

Any Android, Windows, web-capable or SoC smart display works. You can use screens and media players you already own — just install the JioSignage player or open the web player, pair with a code, and you're live.

Can screens keep playing if the internet goes down?

Yes. Content is cached locally on each device, so scheduled playback continues uninterrupted during network outages and syncs automatically once connectivity returns.

How does JioSignage scale across many locations?

The platform is multi-tenant and built for thousands of screens. Group displays by region, brand or franchisee, delegate access with roles, and publish to hundreds of locations in a single action.

Is my data and network secure?

Content is encrypted end-to-end, sign-in supports SSO via SAML/OAuth, and every action is captured in audit logs. Role-based governance ensures teams only access what they should.

Can it integrate with our existing systems?

Open REST APIs let you automate publishing and connect JioSignage to your POS, DAM, data feeds and internal tools. Live web, RSS and data-driven content are supported out of the box.

What kind of support is included?

Enterprise plans include 24/7 support, guided onboarding, an uptime SLA and a dedicated customer success manager to help you roll out and scale.

JioSignage12

What is JioSignage?

JioSignage is a cloud based digital signage platform. Signage is a digital display connected over internet that can be updated easily and remotely at any time. Digital campaign can be scheduled to one to many display at a time from JioSignage platform.

Where can I use JioSignage?

Wherever you can place/hang display and reach potential customers. That’s where you can use JioSignage. The possibilities are endless: Retail, Education, Public Transport, Public Spaces, Restaurants, Hospitality, Factories, Hospitals and more...

Can I run JioSignage directly on Android TV or Android Tablet?

Yes, if you have an Android Smart TV, Android Display or Android Tablet you can directly install the JioSignage app. JioSignage all can be installed on any Android device having OS version 7.0 and above from google play store.

What are the hardware requirement for setting up JioSignage?

Player Requirement ( Any one of option A or B )

  • A. Jio Media Player, HDMI Display
  • B. Android TV/Android Display/Android Tablet
JioSignage support offline content playback?

Yes, JioSignage player support offline content playback. Content playback will continue playing even there is no internet connectivity for already downloaded and scheduled content.

JioSignage supports playback of 4K content?

Yes, 4K video content playback is supported, provided your display supports 4K content playback

JioSignage support grouping of display?

Yes, grouping of display is supported. with few clicks content can be delivered to all the display of groups instantly.

Troubleshooting5

I am getting Device ID Unknown Error, What should I do?

Display ID has been already used on any other device. Please create new display ID and use it.

I am getting can not play this content error, What should I do?

Looks like content may not be downloaded correctly. Please deliver content again on your device.

I am getting application not installed error, What should I do?

JioSignage application may be already installed on your TV/Display, Please uninstall app and reinstall it on your TV/Display.

I am getting Display ID has been already used on other device error, What should I do?

Looks like Display ID has been used on other device, Please create new Display ID from admin portal or JioSignage managment app and use it on your display.

I am getting "parsing error" during application installation, What should I do?

Looks like JioSignage APK not copied correctly to your device. Please copy APK again to your TV/Device and reinstall it.

Display2

What are all media file supported in JioSignage?

All below file formats are supported.

  • Audio: .mp3, OggVorbis, WAV
  • Video: .mp4, Webm & .mov
  • Images: .jpeg, .png, .gif, .jfif
  • Documents: .pdf, .ppt, .pptx .doc, .zip
Is the TV showing "No Signal"/ blue screen ?

Relaunch the application. Clear cache: settings > applications > downloaded > apps> Jio Signage > Clear Data. If still not working, repush the schedule.

Proof of Play2

Is proof of plays available in JioSignage?

Yes, all the content played on screen are recorded with time and how many time it has been played with the display name. Detail report can be downloaded/Emailed.

Can I see what is playing on my display?

Yes, from JioSignage web portal you can request for screen capture of content which is particular display.

Yes, sound track can be added while creating content.

Can I create multiple partition within the display to run different contents?

Yes, JioSignage support split screen. You can create multiple partitions of screen and insert different types of content.

Yes, you can have multiple RSS (News feeds) and rolling text on the same screen.

Can I see the preview of the content how it will look on my actual screen?

Yes, PPT/PDF files can be uploaded. It will be converted to images and converted images can be used to display over your screen.
